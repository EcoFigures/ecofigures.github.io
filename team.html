Who We Are

EcoFigures is built and maintained by the people below. We are a team of undergraduate student researchers led by PI Dr. Robin Decker. We share an interest in improving quantitative literacy in undergraduate ecology courses. Interested in joining the team? Contact us at rrdecker@ucdavis.edu.

Dr. Robin Decker
Principal Investigator
Robin is an Assistant Professor of Teaching in the Department of Evolution and Ecology at the University of California, Davis. Her research focuses on inclusive teaching pedagogies and the development of quantitative reasoning skills in ecology education.
