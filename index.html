EcoFigures

A searchable collection of figures from peer-reviewed ecology literature, built to give students real practice interpreting ecological figures.

Built for instructors who want their students interpreting real ecological data, not just recognizing textbook graphs. Figures sourced from peer-reviewed journals and curated by the EcoFigures team. Contact Robin Decker at rrdecker@ucdavis.edu for questions.
