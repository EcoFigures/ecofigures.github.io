About the Project

EcoFigures is a curated collection of figures from peer-reviewed ecology journals, built to give students structured practice interpreting figures they've never seen before.

The problem we're trying to solve

Undergraduate ecology students often do well on exam questions built around graphs they've already seen in lecture, but struggle to apply the same quantitative skills to unfamiliar figures. Since ecology relies heavily on data and figure interpretation to communicate findings, this is a real barrier to scientific literacy. Instructors know their students need broad exposure to real figures, but building that kind of collection from scratch takes more time than most instructors can spare.

What this database provides

Each entry links to a figure published in a well-recognized, peer-reviewed journal, spanning a wide range of figure types, taxa, and difficulty levels. Entries are tagged so instructors can build practice sets around a specific topic, concept area, or skill level, and each entry comes with practice questions ranging from straightforward data extraction to more demanding interpretation.

How it's been used

This resource has been integrated into an upper-division undergraduate ecology course through active-learning interventions — in-class response questions, homework assignments, and exam problems. Its effectiveness is currently being evaluated with a mixed-methods study, using surveys at the start and end of the quarter to measure changes in students' self-efficacy and quantitative performance. Preliminary results suggest that repeated practice with unfamiliar figures improves students' ability to interpret novelfigures.

Using this resource

This resource is freely available for instructors to use in their own courses. Instructors can build practice sets by searching and filtering the database. The database is designed to be flexible, allowing instructors to tailor assignments to their course objectives and student needs. This resource is free and will remain free for educational use.
